Brain2M

Every voxel has a Manhattan plot. Every variant has a brain map.

Brain2M runs a genome-wide association study at each of 2 million brain voxels, 8 million variants apiece: 1.6 × 10¹³ tests. Click a voxel to read its genome. Click a variant on the ring to see its effect across the brain.
